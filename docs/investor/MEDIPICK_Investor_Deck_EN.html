✚ MEDIPICKCOMPLIANCE BY DESIGN
COMPLIANCE BY DESIGN

Monetize discoverability.
Protect medical information.

Medical Service Act Art. 56, copyright, privacy and Google Maps attribution are hard constraints.

Advertising review

Only permitted advertisers; human review, clear sponsored labels and applicable ad approvals.

Data minimization

No symptom or diagnosis telemetry for advertisers. Location used per opt-in query.

Publishing permissions

Blog ownership proof; metadata and canonical links, not unauthorized full-content copying.
